    Properties

    ReadonlycaCertPath

    caCertPath: PemSource | readonly PemSource[]

    The trusted CA certificates: the root of the signer, and the intermediate CAs if the seal does not embed them.

    Optional Readonlycrls

    crls?: readonly Uint8Array<ArrayBufferLike>[]

    CRLs, in DER or PEM, for the certificates of the signer and of the time-stamping authority. A seal of level LT embeds its own: these are then used besides them, a newer CRL telling a revocation they do not.

    Optional ReadonlyexpectedSubject

    expectedSubject?: string | NameFields

    The subject the signing certificate must have: its one-line DN, or fields that must all match.

    Optional Readonlylimits

    limits?: ZipLimits

    The limits of the reading of the container.

    Optional ReadonlyocspResponses

    ocspResponses?: readonly Uint8Array<ArrayBufferLike>[]

    OCSP responses (DER), likewise.

    Optional Readonlyrevocation

    revocation?: boolean

    Whether the revocation of the certificates is checked (default true). A seal whose revocation cannot be checked is invalid; false verifies it without, which isRevocationChecked() then tells.

    Optional ReadonlytsaCertPath

    tsaCertPath?: PemSource | readonly PemSource[]

    The trusted certificates of the time-stamping authorities: required to verify a time-stamped seal.
