    Module seal

    Seals of files: detached XAdES baseline signatures (ETSI EN 319 132-1) in an ASiC-E container (ETSI EN 319 162-1), at levels B, T and LT. The certificates and the algorithms come from @webcoder31/ezxmldsig/core.

    Experimental in 2.x: its API may change in a minor version.
